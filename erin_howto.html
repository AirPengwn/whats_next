What’s Next for Erin
How to use this site
From “I found something I like” to “I applied and I’m tracking it” — every click, in order.
⚠ Before anything else: use Erin’s laptop.
Only her laptop (the primary device) saves changes. On any other device you’ll see a “View-only” banner across the top — buttons still respond, but everything resets the moment you refresh. To confirm you’re on the right one, open My List and look for “This is the primary device ✓” near the top.
Part 1 — You found an opportunity you like
1
Save it to My List
  1. In the top nav choose Browse, then the matching page — Programs, Internships, Jobs, or Service.
  2. Find the card. Fastest way: type its number (e.g. 0137) or a keyword into the search box at the top.
  3. Click ☆ Save to My List — it changes to ★ Saved.
2
Optional — flag it while you’re there
Neither is required, but both make the long list easier to work through.
  1. ☆ Priority — for the ones she’s serious about. Also saves the item automatically, and pulls it into its own column on the Pipeline.
  2. 👁 Mark reviewed — means “I’ve looked at this.” The card dims, and it disappears from the 👁 Not yet reviewed filter so you never read the same card twice.
  3. ✗ Not interested — hides it from normal views. Nothing is ever deleted; you can always bring it back.
✓ You do NOT need to create an application.
The moment you save something to My List, the site automatically creates a card for it on the Applications Tracker, starting at status “Not started.” It’s already waiting for you.
Part 2 — You applied online
Apply on the organisation’s own website exactly as you normally would — this site doesn’t submit anything for you. Once you’ve submitted there, come back here and record it.
Part 3 — Record it on the Tracker
3
Open the application’s card
  1. Top nav → Track → Tracker.
  2. Find its card — use the ⭐ Priority filter, or search its number (e.g. 0137). Every application shows the opportunity’s number in its title.
4
Set the status to “Submitted”
This is the single most important field — it drives the Pipeline board.
  1. Click the status dropdown at the top-right of the card.
  2. Choose Submitted.
  3. It saves instantly — watch the Status pill in the card’s right-hand sidebar update as you pick it. No Save click needed.
5
Fill in the details
  1. Submitted date — click the date box and pick the day you applied.
  2. Materials checklist — tick what you actually sent (Application submitted, CV / resume updated, SOP finalized, Confirmation email saved…). The progress bar fills as you go.
  3. Portal URL and Portal Login — so she can get back into the application later. Username or email only — never the password.
  4. Notes — confirmation number, who you spoke to, anything worth remembering.
  5. Click Save — a green “Saved ✓” flashes next to the button.
✓ Saves by itself
  • Status dropdown
  • Submitted / Decision dates
  • Materials checkboxes
  • Professor picker
⚠ Needs the Save button
  • Portal URL
  • Portal Login
  • Notes
6
Check it landed
  1. Go to Track → Pipeline — the card should now sit in the Submitted column.
  2. Reload the Tracker page — your status, dates and notes should all still be there. If they are, it synced.

Want to try things without breaking anything?
⚠
TEST mode
A safe sandbox — use the whole site, save nothing.
  1. Click ● Test in the top-right of the header, next to the light/dark button.
  2. Confirm the prompt. The page reloads with a yellow “TEST MODE” banner across the top of every page.
  3. Now use anything — change statuses, save opportunities, tick checklists, add notes, remove items. Everything behaves exactly as it really would.
  4. Click Turn off in the banner (or the Test button again). The page reloads and Erin’s real data comes straight back — everything you did is discarded.
Nothing you do in TEST mode is saved anywhere. It loads Erin’s real current data so you’re working with the genuine article, but every change is held in memory only — nothing is written to the cloud and nothing is written to this computer. Closing the tab also ends it automatically.
It’s also the way to try features that are normally locked on a view-only computer — in TEST mode the Save buttons and status dropdowns become usable so you can see how they work.

Each application card is tinted by whose turn it is — so you can scan the page and see what needs you, without reading a single word.

🔍
Search inside a page
My List and the Tracker each have their own search box at the top — separate from the Search in the header. The header search looks across the whole site; these narrow the list you’re standing on. Type a title, an organisation, or a card number (0039), and it tells you how many matched. Clear the box to get everything back.
Two shortcuts worth knowing
★
Every card has a number
Each opportunity shows a number like #0137. Type 0137 into any search box to jump straight to it — and it’s by far the easiest way to refer to a specific one in conversation.
⇄
Everything links both ways
From an opportunity card, the “On your tracker” links jump to its application and materials. From the application, “View opportunity →” jumps back, and “Apply / View posting ↗” opens the live listing.
▾
See the opportunity without leaving the Tracker
On any application card, click ▸ Opportunity details (next to the links under the title). It expands in place to show the fit rating, deadline, pay, location, what the role actually involves, and its focus areas — then click again to collapse. You keep your spot in the list.
Why two cards per opportunity? Each opportunity has two records behind it: the opportunity (shared — title, description, pay, deadline; refreshed by the weekly sweep) and Erin’s application (private — status, dates, portal login, notes, checklist). They share the same number, so #0039 finds both. Keeping them separate means the weekly update can never overwrite her progress notes.

Nothing on this site is ever deleted — items you pass on are hidden, not removed, and stay in the Archive.
